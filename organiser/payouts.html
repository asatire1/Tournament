Payout account

Connect Stripe to accept entry fees for your tournaments. Fees go straight to your bank account (Uber Padel takes a small platform fee).

How it works
  • You connect a Stripe Connect Express account — takes about 2 minutes.
  • Players pay in pounds via Stripe Checkout on the tournament page.
  • Stripe deposits the entry fees into your bank account (minus Stripe's processing fee and Uber Padel's 5% platform fee).
  • You keep full control — refunds, disputes, and payouts are all managed by you through Stripe.
