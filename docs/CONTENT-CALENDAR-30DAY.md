# UberPadel Prize Money Feature — 30-Day Social Media Content Calendar

**Campaign objective:** Drive awareness, sign-ups, and first-tournament participation around UberPadel's prize money feature.
**Target audience:** UK padel enthusiasts, 25–45, active lifestyle, competitive mindset.
**Launch day:** Day 1.

---

## Hashtag Strategy

### Core tags (use on every post)
- `#padel` `#padeluk` `#uberpadel`

### Prize money tags (use whenever prize money is the main message)
- `#prizemoney` `#winningpadel` `#padellife`

### Discovery / reach tags (rotate 2–3 per post)
- `#padellovers` `#padelplayer` `#padelnation` `#sportsmoney`

### Platform-specific guidance
- **Instagram:** Lead with a visually striking frame in the first 3 seconds of Reels. Use the "save this" CTA on educational carousels to boost ranking. Strong hook text in the caption's first line before the fold.
- **X/Twitter:** Ask a question or make a bold, debatable statement to drive replies. Keep copy under 200 characters where possible. Quote-tweet tournament results with commentary rather than just resharing.
- **Facebook:** Target padel club groups and local sports communities. Frame posts as community events rather than advertising. Use Facebook Events for actual tournaments.
- **TikTok:** Hook in the first 1–2 seconds (on-screen text + voice or music). Trending audio lifts reach significantly. Stitch/duet opportunities from player reaction videos.
