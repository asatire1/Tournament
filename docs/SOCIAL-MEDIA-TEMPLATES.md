# UberPadel — Social Media Post Templates

30 ready-to-use templates grouped into 6 categories. Each template includes:
- Platform suitability badges
- Placeholder variables in `[BRACKETS]`
- A hashtag block

**Tone:** Energetic but professional. UK-focused. Prize money angle front and centre where relevant.

**Platform key:**
- 📸 Instagram
- 🐦 X / Twitter (≤ 280 chars)
- 📘 Facebook
- 🎵 TikTok caption
