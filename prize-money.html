Verified Results. Trusted Pay-outs.

Every prize money tournament goes through a 4-step verification process before any funds are released.

All match scores entered

Every match result must be recorded in the app before the tournament can be submitted for approval. No missing scores are permitted.

2 venue photos submitted

The organiser must upload at least 2 photos taken at the venue on the day — confirming the event took place as registered.

50% player confirmations

At least half of all registered players must confirm their final standings via the app. This prevents unilateral result manipulation.

Admin review within 48 hours

Our admin team reviews every submission, cross-checks evidence, and either approves or queries the results within 48 hours of submission.
